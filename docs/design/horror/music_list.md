# Music list

Files: P:/FoA-Assets/audio/music/processed/ (48 kHz, 16-bit, stereo WAV), previews in previews/ as MP3 (loops play once through and then 15 s past the wrap). Built by Tools/Audio/make_music.py from the sources in user_src/ (the user's picks) and cc_src/.

Each loop is the whole file: the file's last sample flows into its first, so set Looping on the SoundWave and don't add any loop points. "Seam" is the largest spectral change in the 0.2 s across the wrap divided by the 95th percentile of the same measure anywhere in the loop: below 1 means the wrap is smoother than most moments in the music. "Beats" is the same measure at the loop's own beats (99th percentile): a bar-aligned wrap lands on a beat, so it passes if it reads like one of them. "Naive cut" is the same excerpt looped without the crossfade.

Shared treatment, the same for every cue, so they read as one soundtrack and stay subtle, because the voices are the point: tape speed 0.95 (tempo and pitch, about -0.9 semitone), 70 Hz high-pass, 7 kHz low-pass, +1.5 dB at 220 Hz, -2 dB at 3.2 kHz, soft tape saturation, wow 0.2 % and flutter 0.05 %, a dark hall (RT60 2.8 s lows to 0.9 s highs) 10 dB under the dry voice, tape hiss 42 dB under, beds at -23 LUFS and the sting at -18 LUFS, -1 dBTP ceiling. No lyric smearing: the vocals stay intact.
