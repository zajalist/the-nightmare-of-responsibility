# Player character: free routes with commercial rights (researched 2026-11-07)

**Decision:** MPFB2. Reallusion CC5 was dropped because its trial is marked non-commercial; that comes from main's reading of Reallusion's comparison table, which I didn't re-check. See `mpfb_pipeline.md`.

| Route | Licence (primary source) | Quality for a hero teen | Rig | Path into UE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. MPFB2 (MakeHuman for Blender)**, chosen | Code is GPLv3, not shipped. Assets are CC0 1.0, and "the MakeHuman team makes no claim whatsoever over output such as exports to files (FBX ...)" (mpfb2 `LICENSE.md`; static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html). Asset packs are CC0 or CC-BY: check each pack's page. | Realistic, plain faces. Strong body-shape control: age, weight, muscle, height, proportions, hundreds of face and body targets, asymmetry. Card or shell hair. | Built-in `game_engine` rig with 53 bones and UE4-Mannequin names, plus Rigify and a Mixamo-compatible rig. Face uses ARKit-style CC0 shape keys (faceunits01). | FBX per piece on one skeleton, then IK retarget from Manny. CC0 `.mhclo` clothes auto-fit any body shape and hide the skin under them. |
| 2. Blender Studio Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 | CC0 (blender.org/download/demo-files, "Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 ... 49 MB, CC0"). | Base sculpting meshes only: no textures, hair, clothes or rig. Reaching hero quality needs real sculpting, texturing and clothing work. | None. Rigify or Mixamo by hand. | Manual. Clothes would have to be modelled and fitted by hand, which the user ruled out. |
| 3. Lux3D image-to-3D, plus auto-rig | No public terms of use for Lux3D output found. Check the Aholo hackathon and account terms before shipping. | One fused static mesh (body and clothes together) with no face rig. Weak for a close-up face. | Lux3D has its own **character auto-rigging** and **animation retargeting** API (`/animations/rig/task/create`, `/animations/retarget/task/create`, 85 preset motions; cached docs `P:\FoA-Assets\_cache\lux3d\lux3d-docs.md`). Skeleton bone names aren't documented. | GLB, then UE. Strong for the 30% Manycore judging score, but not as the face-on-screen hero. Better for props or secondary figures. |
| 4. Mixamo (auto-rigger and characters) | Adobe's Mixamo FAQ (helpx.adobe.com; it returns 403 to scripts, so I read it through Adobe's community FAQ and search excerpts): free, royalty-free for personal, commercial and non-profit projects, including games. You can't redistribute the raw character or animation files as the product, and it can't be used to train ML models. Adobe ID required. | Stock characters are generic. The auto-rigger needs a T-pose mesh, gives a body rig only, and its fingers are basic. | `mixamorig:` skeleton, about 65 bones, no face. | FBX, then UE, then IK retarget. Useful only as an auto-rigger for an existing mesh. |
