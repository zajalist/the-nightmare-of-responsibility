## 2026-11-09 world lane: story props for the bare desk and the bed wall
- **Tool:** `python Tools/Lux3D/lux3d_batch.py --table docs/design/world/lux3d-world-prompts.md --only "<Item>"` (prompts are the table rows). All G1 text-to-3D, GLB out, 2048 base colour.
- **Why:** the desk is searched at T2 ("Old worksheets. Cold coffee.") and the intro opens on "Seventeen days. Not that anyone's counting.", but the desk was bare and nothing in the room counted days. Each prop below is a line of the script made physical.
- **Cold coffee mug.** Task 5298676, faceCount 10k, customSize 100 mm. Out: `P:\FoA-Assets\lux3d\cold_coffee_mug\textured.glb`, 9.0k tris, 11 x 9.5 x 8.6 cm. Status (15:07): placed as Dress_Lux3D_CoffeeMug on the desk front (`SM_Lux3D_CoffeeMug`).
- **Worksheet stack.** Task 5298699, 15k, 60 mm. Out: `...\worksheet_stack\textured.glb`, 11.1k tris, 28 x 19 x 28 cm; the texture has handwritten lined pages. Status (15:07): placed as Dress_Lux3D_Worksheets at the desk back-left, scaled to a 5 cm stack (`SM_Lux3D_Worksheets`).
- **School backpack.** Task 5298715, 20k, 450 mm. Out: `...\school_backpack\textured.glb`, 20.1k tris, 54 x 48 x 54 cm, green canvas (reads as mid grey in the mono grade). Status (15:07): placed as Dress_Lux3D_Backpack on the floor between desk and commode (`SM_Lux3D_Backpack`).
- **Wall calendar.** Task 5308486, 10k, 450 mm. Out: `...\wall_calendar\textured.glb`, 16.3k tris, 21 x 21 x 2 cm (the size hint was not applied; scaled in engine). The page carries a month grid with pen X marks through the days, which is the "Seventeen days" beat. Status (15:36): placed as `Dress_Lux3D_Calendar` on the door wall (`SM_Lux3D_WallCalendar`). Lux3D scattered its X marks over an atlas, so the count could not be set there: an exact page (17 days crossed, the 18th ringed) drawn by `Tools/Lux3D/make_calendar_page.py` lies over the front (`Dress_Lux3D_Calendar_Page`, `MI_Lux3D_WallCalendar_Page`); the Lux3D mesh still gives the rings, curl and thickness.
- **Hoodie on chair.** Task 5308532, 20k, 700 mm. Out: `...\hoodie_on_chair\textured.glb`, 14.5k tris, 70 x 84 x 34 cm, a grey hoodie that reads hanging, not draped. Status: rejected for placement (it does not sit naturally on the chair back or the door hook); not imported.
- **Texture sheet:** `P:\FoA\Saved\WorldShots\lux3d\story_props_basecolour_sheet.png` (base colours, left to right: mug, hoodie, backpack, calendar, worksheets).
- **Alarm clock hero.** Task 5366589, G1 30k, 140 mm. Out: `P:\FoA-Assets\lux3d\alarm_clock_hero\textured.glb`, 17.0k tris, 12.6 x 8.2 x 16 cm, twin steel bells, a pale dial with bold numerals and a red second hand. Why: at the anim lane's 40-90 cm insert the black splat clock had no readable form. It becomes the intact clock in BP_SandMaterialize (ClockMesh); the black-sand splat copy only shows from the slap and crumbles. The generated dial carried a real maker's wordmark: inpainted off both dial copies into `T_Lux3D_AlarmClock_BC.png` (SOURCES.txt there). Preview: `P:\FoA\Saved\WorldShots\lux3d\alarm_clock_hero_preview.png`. Status (15:27): used as `SM_Lux3D_AlarmClock` (2K), ClockMesh on SandClock_WakeUp, 15 cm tall, dial to the pillow.
- **Smartphone v2 (used).** Task 5395323, G1 15k, 147 mm. Prompt: a generic unbranded slab, matte dark grey frame and back with light scuffs, flat black glass front with a thin bezel, a two-lens camera bump, no logos. Out: `P:\FoA-Assets\lux3d\smartphone_v2\textured.glb`, 13.8k tris (Lux3D gave it at 298 mm; scaled to 147 x 71 mm, thickness x0.8 to ~8.5 mm body, by `Tools/Lux3D/phone_v2_prep.py`). Used by BP_Phone (the T0 phone, the nightstand phone and the intro panel). The previous phone (task 4732080 and its retopo 4732326) is retired. Preview: `P:\FoA\Saved\WorldShots\lux3d\smartphone_v2_preview.png`.
